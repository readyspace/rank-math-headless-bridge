# Credits and open source

## Rank Math

Thank you to the **Rank Math team and contributors** for creating and maintaining
Rank Math SEO, its Headless CMS Support and its Instant Indexing tools.
This bridge relies on that work. Rank Math creates the SEO data; this plugin
helps a separate frontend use it.

- [Rank Math website](https://rankmath.com/)
- [Official Rank Math source](https://github.com/rankmath/seo-by-rank-math)
- [Rank Math Headless CMS Support guide](https://rankmath.com/kb/headless-cms-support/)

Rank Math is installed separately. Its code is not bundled in this project.
This is an independent community integration. These credits do not claim an
endorsement, partnership or support agreement with Rank Math.
